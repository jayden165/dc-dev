Payment

I don't take money.

Right now I'm not charging for my work. Instead of cash, I take payment in the form of perks inside your server — things that help me stay active and visible in the communities I build for.

Paid ranks

A paid or premium rank in your server, granted for the duration of the project and beyond.

Custom channels

A dedicated channel set up for me, with the permissions I need. I use these channels to advertise my services.

@everyone / @here pings

The ability to ping @everyone or @here for announcements related to the work.

Recognition

Credit as the developer, a staff/dev role, and a shout-out to your community.

  • Exact perks are flexible — we'll agree on what works for your server before I start.
  • This may change in the future, but for now every build is done for perks, not payment.
  • I can't do everything — what I take on is limited by my experience and my schedule.
  • Bigger or more complex projects may ask for a bit more in return.

FAQ

Questions people ask me.

How do I hire you?

Send a request above or message me on Discord and tell me about your server and what you need. I'll let you know if I can take it on.

Do you work for free?

I don't take money right now. I work in return for perks in your server, like a paid rank, a custom channel, pings or recognition. We agree on it before I start.

What do you need from me?

A clear idea of what you want, access to the server for the work, and a way to reach you on Discord.

What can you build?

Custom bots with slash commands, moderation and ticket systems, verification, leveling, economies, giveaways, reaction roles, and full server setups.

Can you also set up a Minecraft server?

Yes. Check my Minecraft developer site for the servers I have built and managed.

How fast do you reply?

I'm very active on Discord and usually reply quickly. Discord is the best way to reach me.
